### DEV Community (technical article / project showcase)

**Title:** What I learned shipping a local Windows AI image upscaler—and where its results fail

I built Biya Upscale as a free portable Windows app for local 2× and 4× Real-ESRGAN image upscaling. Images stay on the computer during inference; the model must be downloaded once first. The release is a ZIP containing the full application folder, not an installer. I have verified DirectML on an NVIDIA RTX 2050 only, so I’m not claiming that every GPU or PC is tested.

The quality trade-off matters: AI can add or alter details, and small text/numbers in screenshots can become less legible. I’m looking for 5–10 Windows users to test extraction/launch, first model download, CPU/GPU selection, speed at both scales, output quality, and crashes. Please do not share private images or sensitive logs.

- Release: https://github.com/Biya-dev/Biya-Upscale/releases/tag/v1.0.0
- Feedback checklist: https://github.com/Biya-dev/Biya-Upscale/issues/1

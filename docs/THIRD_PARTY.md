# Third-party components & licenses

Biya Upscale is **MIT** licensed (see `LICENSE`). The app runs entirely on the
user's machine: there are no paid APIs, no cloud inference, and no per-user
costs. The third-party components below are all free/open licenses.

## AI models (downloaded on first launch — not bundled)

| Model | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| Real-ESRGAN x4 Plus (`real_esrgan_x4.onnx`) | [SceneWorks/real-esrgan-onnx](https://huggingface.co/SceneWorks/real-esrgan-onnx) — ONNX export of official Real-ESRGAN `RealESRGAN_x4plus` weights, verified parity vs torch reference | [BSD-3-Clause](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/blob/master/LICENSE) |
| Real-ESRGAN x2 Plus (`real_esrgan_x2.onnx`) | [SceneWorks/real-esrgan-onnx](https://huggingface.co/SceneWorks/real-esrgan-onnx) — ONNX export of official Real-ESRGAN `RealESRGAN_x2plus` weights | [BSD-3-Clause](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/blob/master/LICENSE) |
| Real-ESRGAN General x4 v3, fast (`realesr-general-x4v3.onnx`) | [CoderViking/realesr-general-x4v3-onnx](https://huggingface.co/CoderViking/realesr-general-x4v3-onnx) — ONNX export with documented provenance (official `realesr-general-x4v3.pth`, SHA-256 cited) | [BSD-3-Clause](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/blob/master/LICENSE) |

The original architecture and weights come from the
[Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) project by Xintao Wang
et al. (Tencent ARC Lab), paper
[“Real-ESRGAN: Training Real-World Blind Super-Resolution with Pure Synthetic Data”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833)
(ICCVW 2021). If you use Biya Upscale's outputs in research, please cite that paper:

```bibtex
@InProceedings{wang2021realesrgan,
    author    = {Xintao Wang and Liangbin Xie and Chao Dong and Ying Shan},
    title     = {Real-ESRGAN: Training Real-World Blind Super-Resolution with Pure Synthetic Data},
    booktitle = {International Conference on Computer Vision Workshops (ICCVW)},
    date      = {2021}
}
```

BSD-3-Clause requires that redistributions reproduce the copyright notice and
disclaimer — both are reproduced in `docs/BSD-3-Clause-Real-ESRGAN.txt`.
